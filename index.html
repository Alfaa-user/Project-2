The Idea Behind This Website

This website was created as part of my web development learning process. It is a project where I explore HTML, CSS, JavaScript, web design, and interactive elements while learning how to build a complete website from scratch. All characters, stories, locations, coffee farms, and businesses featured on this website are fictional and created solely for educational and portfolio purposes. The project is inspired by the idea of local coffee production in Wonosobo, but it does not represent real farmers, businesses, or coffee producers.

The people behind the beans

Daniel Carter

The Craft of Origin

The Craft of Origin Daniel believes great coffee begins with the land and the patience behind it. He focuses on preserving the natural character of each bean, valuing quality, tradition, and exclusivity over mass production. For Daniel, coffee is not just something to drink, but a story that should be experienced from farm to cup.

Marcus William

Coffee for Everyone

Marcus believes coffee should be accessible to everyone. He focuses on consistency, freshness, and efficient production so his beans can reach more people without losing their character. For Marcus, coffee is not about exclusivity, but about creating a good cup that anyone can enjoy, anywhere.

Arif Rahman

The Patince of The Land

Arif believes great coffee comes from patience, consistency, and understanding the land. He focuses on maintaining healthy plants, careful harvesting, and preserving the balance of his coffee farm. For Arif, coffee is not about producing as much as possible, but about giving every bean enough time to become something worth remembering.

Sinta Wulandari

The Curiosity Behind the Bean

Sinta believes coffee farming should never stop evolving. She explores different cultivation, processing, and roasting methods to discover what Wonosobo coffee can become. For Sinta, every harvest is an opportunity to experiment, learn from mistakes, and find new ways to bring the character of the land into every cup.
